Teaching

Learn the language of quantitative biology.

Our courses reflect how we train in the lab: begin with a biological question, make the model explicit, and move fluently between experiment and computation.

2022–2025

Biophysics 294

Graduate teaching in quantitative approaches to modern biological data, with an emphasis on making biological questions mathematically explicit.

2025–present

Systems Biology 200: Introduction to Modern Systems Biology

A new course connecting high-dimensional measurements, machine learning, automatic differentiation, and large mechanistic models in biology.
